Questions

Before you book.

What exactly do you do?

Proper optimization first, then lead generation. I optimize your Upwork and LinkedIn profiles so they rank and convert, then generate leads through Upwork bidding and my GTM automation across email, Reddit, Instagram and LinkedIn. I'm also learning technical sales closing so I can take deals all the way to signed.

What is Agentic Force?

My full GTM setup, built on n8n, Make.com, GoHighLevel and AI agents. It finds buyers on LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, Reddit and Instagram, enriches them with verified emails, sends personal email sequences from warmed inboxes, and runs an AI sales agent that answers replies, handles objections, books calls and sends proposals. Everything is logged in your CRM. You can watch a demo run in the GTM Lab.

Why don't you scrape Upwork?

Upwork has no public API for this and doesn't allow scraping, so I keep Upwork work manual: I read and bid on posts myself. The automated lead finding runs on platforms that support it.

Can you work with our engineers on scoping?

Yes. I study Software Engineering and know MERN, Laravel, REST APIs, n8n, Make.com and GoHighLevel well enough to scope a project with your dev team before a proposal goes out.

Which tools do you use?

Upwork, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo.io, HubSpot CRM, Salesforce (basic), ClickUp and Fiverr.

Are you open to full-time roles?

Yes. I'm open to SDR, BDR and business development roles at AI and software companies, as well as project-based work.

How do we start?

Book a 15-minute call. Tell me what you sell and who you sell to, and I'll tell you honestly whether I can help.

The full setup

Six layers, one pipeline.

What I build when you hire me. Each layer runs on n8n, Make.com, GoHighLevel and AI agents, and plugs into the tools you already pay for.

1

Presence

A page worth visiting
  • Headline, About and Featured rewritten
  • 3 posts a week on a content plan
  • Services and proof up front
2

Find

Buyers with intent
  • ICP filters on Sales Navigator and Apollo
  • Reddit threads where buyers ask for help
  • Instagram business accounts by niche and activity
3

Enrich

Know who you're writing to
  • Waterfall lookup for the decision maker
  • Verified work emails to keep bounces low
  • Signals: hiring, funding, new locations
4

Email

Inbox, not the spam folder
  • Separate sending domains, warmed inboxes
  • 30 to 40 sends per inbox a day
  • 3 to 4 step sequences, personal first lines
5

AI sales agent

Every reply answered in minutes
  • Trained on your offer, FAQs and tone
  • Handles objections, qualifies on BANT
  • Books the call or sends proposal and contract
  • Hands anything sensitive to you
6

CRM
